## Product name
VOD Coach

## Short description
AI coach for your VALORANT and League of Legends match recordings. Get timestamped feedback on every mistake and good play, tailored to your agent or champion and rank.

## Description
VOD Coach watches your full VALORANT or League of Legends match and coaches you like a real analyst.

Drop in a recording from OBS, Outplayed, Medal or a Twitch VOD, pick your agent or champion and your rank, and the coach reviews the whole game. Every mistake and good play gets a clickable timestamp, an explanation of what happened, and what you should have done instead.

TIMESTAMPED COACHING
Click any note and the video jumps straight to that moment. See exactly where you over-peeked, wasted an ability, missed a rotation or mismanaged a wave.

KNOWS YOUR KIT
VOD Coach knows the real abilities of every VALORANT agent and League of Legends champion, kept up to date automatically, so its advice is specific to what you're playing, like when to use your dash or when to trade around your lane opponent's cooldowns.

COACHING AT YOUR LEVEL
Advice is pitched at your rank, from Iron to Radiant or Challenger, so you work on what will actually get you to the next rank. Tell it what to focus on, like "my dash usage" or "I die to ganks a lot".

IT LEARNS FROM YOU
Disagree with a note? Click Disagree and say why. Your corrections are remembered and used in future reviews, so the coach stops repeating advice that doesn't fit how you play.

FREE TO USE
VOD Coach uses your own free Google Gemini API key. The app walks you through getting one in about a minute, with no credit card. It shows how many free reviews you have left each day.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Your key, settings and reports stay on your PC. Videos are only sent to Google Gemini for analysis and deleted right after.

Requires a Google account to create a free Gemini API key. Free daily limits are set by Google.

- Deeper, harsher reviews for Diamond and above
- New Send feedback button
- When the coaches are busy, the review keeps retrying for a few minutes instead of stopping right away
